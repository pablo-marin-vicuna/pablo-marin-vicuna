# Pablo Marín Vicuña

AI R&D Manager at Banco Falabella Chile. Co-founder & CTO at [Skinology](https://skinology.cl), a Chilean D2C skincare startup with personalized, AI-designed formulas.

20 years leading data, analytics and AI teams in banking, insurance, airlines and telecoms.

## What I work on

- **Banco Falabella:** applied AI R&D and internal consulting. I find processes that could be faster or simpler, prototype the improvement quickly and help take it to production, with the tool the problem needs: machine learning, optimization, speech, vision, LLMs or plain automation. I also run AI adoption workshops across the countries we operate in.
- **Skinology:** I built the platform end to end. An AI engine turns a skin quiz and photos into a personalized formula, checked against dermatologist rules and sent to the lab to be made. Around it: WhatsApp agents for customer care, apps for customers, dermatologists and partner pharmacies and clinics, and the analytics the team runs the business on.
- **Side projects** for the people around me: a music player my nephews and nieces love (songs, stories and characters dancing in sync), a puzzles app my parents and aunt can read and print easily, and a bike trainer I built instead of paying for a subscription.

What drives me is building something useful for someone, and I enjoy the building itself.
